Treasury Payment Anomaly & Fraud Triage

Upload a payment/disbursement CSV (or load the bundled sample) and this tool statistically flags transactions worth a closer look — duplicate payments, outlier amounts, off-hours processing, brand-new vendors with large first payments, and amounts sitting just under a reporting threshold — each with a plain-English explanation. All analysis runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.
